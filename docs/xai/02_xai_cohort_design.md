# XAI Cohort Design

## Purpose
To document the deterministic cohort sampling protocol that guarantees balanced, representative, and target-independent data slices for computing explanations via SHAP and Permutation Importance during the 2019 validation and eventually the 2020 final test phase.

## Scientific Question
How can we ensure that model explanations are computed over comparable, unbiased sub-samples across all configurations without being computationally prohibitive?

## Frozen Inputs
- **Year:** 2019-01-01 to 2019-12-31 (development).
- **Target Stratification:** Forbidden.
- **Balancing Dimensions:** `month` and `spatial_fold`.
- **SHAP Cohort Size:** 50,000.
- **Permutation Cohort Size:** 20,000 (nested deterministic subset).
- **Random Seed:** 42.

## Cohort Generation Rules
Cohorts are generated by establishing deterministic `month x spatial_fold` cells. The process enforces:
1. Target-independent sampling to maintain the authentic distributions of the source data.
2. Equal sample distribution across available cells, maximally uniformly distributing exact row counts (spread equal to 1).
3. Preservation of all canonical key indices (`date`, `y_index`, `x_index`, or `gleam_y_index`, `gleam_x_index`).

## Defined Cohorts
- **SOURCE Native CDF:** `source_cdf_native_2019.parquet` (50,000 rows).
- **SOURCE Native NoCDF:** `source_nocdf_native_2019.parquet` (50,000 rows).
- **SOURCE Matched:** `source_matched_2019.parquet` (50,000 rows) - unified common support cohort used strictly for comparative CDF vs. NoCDF attribution.
- **TRANSFER:** `transfer_2019.parquet` (50,000 rows) - verified single identical support cohort used dynamically across all 4 delta targets (`delta_rzsm`, `delta_et`, `delta_surface_runoff`, `delta_baseflow`) and 2 experiments.
- **VALUE:** `value_2019.parquet` (50,000 rows) - verified single identical GLEAM-grid cohort across experiments.
